## Weekly review
Compare the Life DashBoard sheet, the Weekly/Snapshot tabs of Life OS Data, Goals & Life Direction and Relationships. Reply:
- Win: one thing that went well, with the number or fact.
- Pattern to fix: one recurring problem, with evidence.
- Commitment: one specific action for next week with day and time. Create it as a Calendar event or Task.
If a doc or tab is empty, say which and continue. Do not estimate numbers.
